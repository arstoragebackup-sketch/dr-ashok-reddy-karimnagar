About Dr Ashok Reddy

Dr Ashok Reddy is a hand surgeon based in Karimnagar. He completed his MBBS, followed by DNB training in Mumbai and the MCh in Delhi. His practice is dedicated to surgery of the hand and upper limb — treatment of hand injuries and fractures, repair of tendons, nerves and blood vessels, microvascular reconstruction, and surgery for hand conditions and deformities.

He consults at AR Plastic Surgery, Ratna Nilayam, Jyothinagar, Karimnagar. Hand injuries are often time-critical — for a consultation or an injury, call +91 91826 56866.

Hand Surgery Services

🩹

Hand Injuries & Fractures

Surgical care of cuts and crush injuries of the hand, fixation of hand, finger and wrist fractures, and fingertip and nail bed repair.

🧬

Tendon, Nerve & Artery Repair

Repair of cut flexor and extensor tendons, digital and major nerve repair, and artery repair to restore movement, sensation and blood flow.

🔬

Microvascular Surgery

Surgery under magnification — finger and thumb replantation, revascularization after crush or cut injuries, and free flap reconstruction.

✋

Hand Conditions & Deformity

Carpal tunnel and trigger finger release, ganglion cyst removal, Dupuytren's contracture surgery, deformity correction and hand arthritis surgery.
